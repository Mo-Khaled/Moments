## Exposure, plainly
- **Every product image (1,650 files, about 795 MB) has unknown rights.** If any came from a brand's site, a reseller or a database, the store is exposed to a takedown or claim for those files.
- **Known problem:** the gift bag images (Afnan, Ibraq, Assaf and others) carry another shop's watermark (7 of 8 checked with OCR). They must be replaced before publishing; they are already kept off the homepage, and the vendor name shows as MOMENTS (display only).
- **Highest hint density:** Armaf (26 of 79 images), Arabiyat Sugar (13 of 15), Khadlaj (19 of 306). Hints are weak evidence; they only say where to look first.
- **Lowest risk:** images you shot yourself, and images a brand or distributor sends with written permission (see the request in media-kit-request.md).
- Nothing in this repository downloads or uses brand or retailer photos. The six normalisation samples use the store's own files and stay in `docs/assets/`, not on the store.
