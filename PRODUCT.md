# MOMENTS: product context

register: brand

## What it is
MOMENTS (momentseg.com) is a fragrance store in Egypt. English and Arabic (RTL), prices in EGP shown as "LE 2,250.00",
cash on delivery, mostly mobile shoppers. Fragrances are organised by the moment they are worn (occasion-based set, 2026-10-30):
Office, Evening out, Date night, Summer heat, Weddings & Eid, Winter nights. Gift is a separate collection (menu and hero), not a moment.
The set lives in snippets/moments-config.liquid; each moment is the manual collection <key>-moment (daily, night, love, fresh, occasions, winter).

## Who it is for
Shoppers in Egypt choosing a fragrance for themselves or as a gift, usually on a phone.

## Brand
Plum #3B1E3A, deep plum #251224, berry #B7406A (the only action colour), nude #E9CFC8, pearl #FAF6F2, taupe #75665E. No gold (removed 2026-10-30: accents are nude on dark, berry-ink on light).
One face per script (2026-10-30): Marcellus for Latin, El Messiri for Arabic (OFL, assets/el-messiri-arabic.woff2; stands in for Mirsal, which has no public source).
Six object marks (no faces): office briefcase, evening lantern, date-night rose, summer palm leaf, weddings & Eid crescent and star, winter steaming cup (gift ribbon bottle and sun over waves kept for older content).
